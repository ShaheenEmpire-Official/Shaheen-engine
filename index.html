What this engine can and cannot do

Works now: searches the built-in directory and your private local index; ranks matches; filters by category; creates a local research digest from matching descriptions; validates and saves URLs; imports and exports JSON; stores bookmarks and optional history on this device.

Not included: live global crawling, automatic webpage-content extraction, real-time news feeds, image/video crawling, generative AI answers, or a guarantee of absolute security. These need infrastructure or model capabilities that cannot be created by browser-only code without external services.

URL check: adding a URL verifies its format only. Browsers block a static page from freely reading most other websites, so this file does not claim that a site is online or safe just because its URL is valid.
